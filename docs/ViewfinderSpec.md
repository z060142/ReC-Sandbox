# Viewfinder Spec - frame guides and focus peakingStatus: APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION (2026-08-25). Author: main session.Repository: `E:\coding3\ReC_sandbox\CRYENGINE_Source`, branch `dev`.## What it isOperator aids drawn over the final frame, the way a camera's viewfinder or a monitor does:- **Frame guides**: rule-of-thirds lines, centre cross, action/title safe areas, and an  aspect-ratio framing guide (lines or a translucent shade for a target aspect such as 2.39:1  while the viewport is 16:9 - different from the letterbox, which is part of the picture).- **Focus peaking**: edges that are inside the depth of field are tinted, so the operator sees  what is sharp without judging blur on a small monitor.Both are OVERLAYS: they are never part of the picture, they are editor-live so framing canbe judged while authoring, and they must be trivially switchable off for recording.Everything lives in the PostAA composite (`PostAA.cfx` `PostAAComposites_PS`, after theletterbox mask - the last thing before grain/output), driven by cvars the component pushes.Build with the bundled CMake (targets `CryRenderD3D11` then `CinematicCamera`, Profile,sequential). Zero errors; zero warnings from our files. Do NOT deploy or commit. English only.---## A.
