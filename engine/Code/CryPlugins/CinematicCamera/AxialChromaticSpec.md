# Axial (Longitudinal) Chromatic Aberration ??SpecificationStatus: approved for implementation, 2026-08-26. Owner of semantics: the camera manager.Companion to LensFlawsSpec.md (lateral CA lives there). No new render pass.## 1. What this addsLateral CA is a magnification difference (edges of the frame). Axial CA is a **focus**difference: each wavelength has its own focal plane, so every out-of-focus disc is a differentsize per channel. The look everyone knows from fast and vintage glass ("bokeh fringing"):* highlights **behind** the focus plane carry a **green** rim,* highlights **in front** of it carry a **magenta / purple** rim,* the centre of the frame is affected exactly like the edges (it is not a field effect),* it is a focus error, so its visibility rides the circle of confusion and stopping down hides it.One parameter, `Axial Chromatic Aberration` (0..1). Bold at 1: the shrunk channels' discs are65% the size of the full one ??a fat coloured rim. Natural ~0.2??.4.## 2. ModelEach channel is convolved with its own disc. Rather than three gathers, the gather kernel staysat the layer's CoC and every tap's colour is multiplied by a **per-channel radial mask**: achannel whose disc is smaller only accepts taps inside its own radius. Per-channel weightaccumulation then normalises each channel over the taps it actually took, so energy per channelis conserved and the rim carries only the wider channel's colour. Exactly what a smaller discdoes, at the cost of a few multiplies per tap and no extra samples.Which channels shrink (empirical rule of undercorrected glass):| Layer | Shrunk channels | Rim colour ||---|---|---|| Far (behind focus) | R and B, to `s = 1 - K繚amount` | green || Near (in front) | G, to `s = 1 - K繚amount` | magenta |`K = 0.35`. `s` is in pupil units (1 = the kernel's full radius). Not aperture scaled: theratio between the channels' discs is a property of the glass; the discs themselves alreadyscale with the CoC.Per-tap mask, one-sided outward so that `s = 1` is an exact identity for every tap of the unitpupil: `m(?) = saturate((s - ?) / 0.08 + 1)` with `? = |x|`, `x` the tap in unsqueezed pupilspace BEFORE coma/astigmatism (the same `x` FieldPupilSpec 禮4.2 clips with the barrel).## 3.
