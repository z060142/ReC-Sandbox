# CinematicCamera Console ReferenceConsole commands registered by the plugin, plus the renderer cvars the Cinematic Camera systemrelies on (registered in `Code/CryEngine/RenderDll/Common/RendererCVars.cpp`).The cvars fall into two groups:- **Live tuning (character)**: values used in normal operation. Note that  `r_HDRVignetteAmount`, `r_LensDistortion` and `r_LensLetterbox` are **overwritten every  frame** while a cinematic camera drives the render state (from its Vignette / Distortion /  Anamorphic Squeeze properties), so  setting them by hand only sticks while no cinematic camera is active; the plugin restores  the pre-activation values when the camera stops driving.- **Diagnostic**: force modes used to verify that a pipeline stage runs at all, independent of  what the game code feeds it.Naming standard: every depth-of-field cvar spells the family out as `r_DepthOfField*`. Threecvars were renamed to follow it:| Old name | Current name || --- | --- || `r_DofBokehShapeStrength` | `r_DepthOfFieldBokehShapeStrength` || `r_DofSpriteBokehDebug` | `r_DepthOfFieldSpriteBokehDebug` || `r_DofResolutionScale` | `r_DepthOfFieldResolutionScale` |(The C++ members keep their short `CV_r_Dof*` names; only the console-facing names changed.)## Console commands### ccam_slot```ccam_slot <1-9>```Activates the cinematic camera whose **Camera Slot** property equals the given number, whichis the same ownership transfer as the Schematyc `Activate` function. It logs the entity itswitched to (`[CinematicCamera] slot 3 -> 'CamB'`) or reports that no cinematic camera carriesthat slot. Called with no argument it prints its usage line.A Phone Tracker with **Follow Active Camera** enabled follows every switch, re-anchoring onthe new camera and re-sending the last lens state to it.Bind the slots to the number keys to cut between cameras during play:```bind 1 ccam_slot 1bind 2 ccam_slot 2bind 3 ccam_slot 3```The same switch is exported for other plugins as `CryActivateCinematicCameraSlot(int slot)`(see `Interface/ICinematicCameraOptics.h`).## Live tuning### r_DepthOfFieldMode| | || --- | --- || Default | 1 || Purpose | Selects the DOF implementation. 1 = pure gather. 0 = hybrid: the same gather runs first, then a highlight splat layer (sprite bokeh) is added on top of it. |Driven by the component's **Use Sprite Bokeh** property (on -> 0), saved and restored aroundthe camera being active. Setting it to 0 from the console is an equally valid way to reach thehybrid path — the shape texture follows the effective cvar, not the property.### r_DepthOfFieldBokehShapeStrength| | || --- | --- || Default | 2.0 || Range | clamped to 0.25-4.0 in use || Purpose | Exaggeration exponent for the polygonal bokeh kernel. 1 = physical radius modulation (very subtle at high blade counts: 19% for a pentagon, 8% for an octagon, nearly invisible after the quarter-resolution gather and TAA); 2-3 = clearly readable polygons; 4 = star-like caricature. |Not overwritten by the plugin; tune it from the console at any time. Blade Curvature feeds thesame exponent: straight blades (curvature 0) run it at double strength and stay fully formed atevery aperture.### r_DepthOfFieldResolutionScale| | || --- | --- || Default | 0 || Purpose | Internal resolution of the hybrid splat pass (`r_DepthOfFieldMode 0`). 0 = half-resolution quad grid (cheapest; highlight selection at 2x2 pixel granularity). 1 = native-resolution grid, every native pixel its own splat candidate (sharpest highlight selection; ~4x the DOF vertex work, ~12M vertices at 1080p, early-outs keep the surviving work sparse). 2 = reserved for a native-resolution gather as well; currently behaves as 1. |Splat energy is normalised per grid tier, so the same Highlight Splat Gain deposits the sameenergy at any of these settings.### r_HDRVignetteAmount| | || --- | --- || Default | 0.0 || Range | clamped to 0.0-4.0 in use || Purpose | Strength of the tone map stage's vignette map. 0 = off (default), 1 = the stock map, 2 = deepened. |**Overwritten every frame while a cinematic camera is active** (result of Vignette Amount +Vignette Aperture Link), in edit mode as well as in game mode.### r_LensDistortion| | || --- | --- || Default | 0.0 || Range | clamped to -0.9-0.9 in use || Purpose | Radial lens distortion in the final composite (PostAA). Positive = barrel, negative = pincushion / wide-angle correction. |**Overwritten every frame while a cinematic camera is active** (result of Distortion +Distortion Focal Link; that result is already multiplied by 0.35, so the written value stayswithin +/-0.35), in edit mode as well as in game mode.### r_LensLetterbox| | || --- | --- || Default | 0.0 || Purpose | Aspect ratio to letterbox the final frame to (PostAA): black bars top and bottom when the frame is wider than the viewport. 0 = off. The visible band is `viewportAspect / letterboxAspect` of the frame height, hard-edged like a real gate mask. A **negative** value is the squeezed presentation (Cinematic Camera "Show Squeezed Frame"): the same band is stretched vertically to fill the viewport instead of masked - the raw anamorphic negative, where the whole picture carries the squeeze. |**Overwritten every frame while a cinematic camera is active**, in edit mode as well as in gamemode: the desqueezed anamorphic format aspect (`sensorWidth * Squeeze / sensorHeight`) whilethat format is wider than the viewport, 0 otherwise. Setting it by hand for a plain 2.39:1 masktherefore only sticks while no cinematic camera is driving the render state.### Viewfinder overlaysEight cvars drive the viewfinder overlays drawn at the end of the PostAA composite. They are**overlays: never part of the picture**, and they are **overwritten every frame while acinematic camera is active** (from its Viewfinder property group), in edit mode as well as ingame mode - so setting them by hand only sticks while no cinematic camera is driving the renderstate. The plugin restores the pre-activation values when the camera stops driving.| Cvar | Type | Default | Purpose || --- | --- | --- | --- || `r_LensGuides` | int (bitmask) | 0 | Framing guides: 1 = rule of thirds, 2 = centre cross, 4 = action safe (90%) + title safe (80%) rectangles, 8 = aspect framing guide. 0 = off. || `r_LensGuideAspect` | float | 2.39 | Target aspect for the framing guide (bit 8): the band of that aspect centred in the viewport is outlined and the outside is shaded. || `r_LensGuideOpacity` | float | 0.6 | Line opacity, 0-1. The shading outside the aspect band is half of it. || `r_LensPeaking` | float | 0.0 | Focus peaking strength. Edge response inside the depth of field is tinted with this weight. 0 = off. || `r_LensPeakingNear` | float | 0.0 | Near limit of the in-focus band, metres. Pushed by the component from its thin-lens DOF bounds, every frame. || `r_LensPeakingFar` | float | 0.0 | Far limit of the in-focus band, metres. || `r_LensPeakingColor` | int | 1 | 1 = red, 2 = green, 3 = blue, 4 = yellow, 5 = white. || `r_LensOverlays` | int | 1 | Master switch for ALL viewfinder overlays (guides + peaking). |**`r_LensOverlays 0` is the recording kill switch**: it forces the guide bitmask and the peakingstrength to zero in the renderer, so no overlay can reach the frame regardless of what the otherseven cvars or the component's properties say. The component itself sets it to 1 only while`gEnv->IsEditing()` is true or its **Overlays In Game Mode** property is on, so a recording madein game mode is clean by default.### r_MotionBlurShutterSpeed| | || --- | --- || Default | 250.0 || Purpose | Motion blur exposure time as 1/x seconds. |Driven by the plugin while the component's **Enable Motion Blur** is on: set to the Camera Body`Shutter (1/x s)` denominator (1/50 = the classic 180 degree film look), restored when thecamera stops driving. `r_MotionBlur` is set to 2 if it was 0.## Diagnostic### r_DepthOfFieldBokehQuality| | || --- | --- || Default | 0 || Purpose | 0 = normal (bokeh shape driven by the `Dof_User_ApertureBlades` / `Dof_User_FNumber` params). **N >= 3 = force mode**: an N-blade polygonal kernel at full blend and fully curved blades, independent of any game code. 3 (triangle) is the easiest way to verify the kernel shape is live. |The force mode also keeps the polygon kernel when a shape mask would otherwise take over —diagnostics outrank the mask. Kernel parameter changes are logged as`[DOF] kernel blades=N fNumber=X`, with `(FORCED)` appended in force mode.### r_DepthOfFieldSpriteBokehDebug| | || --- | --- || Default | 0 || Purpose | Diagnostic for the hybrid sprite bokeh splats (`r_DepthOfFieldMode 0`). 1 = every surviving splat is drawn as its raw shape mask at full strength, skipping the energy normalization, so the silhouette (texture x iris polygon) becomes unmissable. 0 = off. |### r_DepthOfFieldSpriteBokehFadeCoC| | || --- | --- || Default | 1.0 || Purpose | Circle of confusion (gather units; roughly 16 px of disc diameter per unit at 1080p) at which a hybrid sprite bokeh splat reaches full strength. Splats fade in over one third to one times this value and are skipped below it. The gather's halo loses energy as a highlight approaches focus while an energy-conserving splat only gets brighter per pixel, so without the fade a small sharp silhouette floats on an already-converged light and then vanishes in a single frame. Lower it to keep shapes on smaller discs, raise it if shapes still pop near focus. The splat also sizes itself from the gather's own CoC texture, so both paths agree on how far out of focus a highlight is. |### r_HDRStreaks| | || --- | --- || Default | 1 || Purpose | Master switch for the aperture diffraction streaks (sunstars), driven by the `HDR_Streaks_*` post effect params and composited into the bloom target. 0 = off (no passes issued), 1 = on (visibility decided by the game-side params), **2 = diagnostic force mode**: full strength, low threshold (0.5), eight spikes, fixed angle, independent of the game-side params — use it to verify the streak pass itself runs. |### r_HDRHalation| | || --- | --- || Default | 1 || Purpose | Master switch for film halation — the red-orange halo around sources that overload the emulsion — driven by the `HDR_Halation_*` post effect params and composited into the bloom target right after the streaks. 0 = off (no passes issued), 1 = on (visibility decided by the game-side params), **2 = diagnostic force mode**: amount 1, threshold 0.5, radius 0.7, independent of the game-side params — use it to verify the halation pass itself runs. |## Related post effect params (for reference)Written by the plugin every frame; not cvars, and not meant to be driven by hand:- `Dof_Active` (forced to 0 while the component drives DOF, then restored),  `Dof_User_Active`, `Dof_User_FocusDistance`, `Dof_User_FocusRange`, `Dof_User_BlurAmount`,  `Dof_User_ApertureBlades`, `Dof_User_FNumber`, `Dof_User_BladeRotation`,  `Dof_User_BladeCurvature`, `Dof_User_MaskFNumber`, `Dof_User_MaskRotation` (radians,  independent of the iris phase), `Dof_User_HighlightThreshold`,  `Dof_User_HighlightGain`, `Dof_User_AnamorphicSqueeze` (1 = spherical; above 1 the gather  taps and the splat quads are narrowed horizontally, so bokeh becomes vertical ovals)- `Dof_User_BokehShapeTex` (string param; rewritten every frame so both render command buffer  slots converge, unbound on restore)- `FilterGrain_Amount` (combined with the TOD environment grain by `max()`)- `HDR_Streaks_Active` / `HDR_Streaks_Amount` / `HDR_Streaks_Count` / `HDR_Streaks_Angle` /  `HDR_Streaks_Length` (unknown to an unmodified renderer: reads return 0, writes are silently  dropped)- `HDR_Streaks_AnamorphicAmount` / `HDR_Streaks_AnamorphicLength` /  `HDR_Streaks_AnamorphicThreshold` / `HDR_Streaks_AnamorphicTint` (Vec4, rgb) — the  anamorphic horizontal flare. Pushed whenever the camera drives the render state, including  while **Enable Diffraction Streaks** is off: the flare belongs to the front element, not to  the iris. `r_HDRStreaks` is still the master switch for both.- `HDR_Halation_Amount` / `HDR_Halation_Threshold` / `HDR_Halation_Radius` /  `HDR_Halation_Tint` (Vec4, rgb) — film halation. Overwritten every frame straight from the  component's Halation group, exactly like the streak params, whenever the camera drives the  render state; nothing here is modulated by the f-number, because halation belongs to the  film stock rather than to the lens. Unknown to an unmodified renderer (reads return 0,  writes are silently dropped). Master switch: `r_HDRHalation`.- `E3DPARAM_HDR_EYEADAPTATION_PARAMS` (exposure) and  `E3DPARAM_HDR_COLORGRADING_COLOR_BALANCE` (white balance) are global 3D engine parameters,  written from the `UpdateBeforeFinalizeCamera()` hook so the Time of Day system cannot  overwrite them in the same frame.
