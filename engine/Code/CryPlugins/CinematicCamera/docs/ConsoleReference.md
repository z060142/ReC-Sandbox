# CinematicCamera Console ReferenceConsole commands registered by the plugin, plus the renderer cvars the Cinematic Camera systemrelies on (registered in `Code/CryEngine/RenderDll/Common/RendererCVars.cpp`).The cvars fall into two groups:- **Live tuning (character)**: values used in normal operation. Note that  `r_HDRVignetteAmount`, `r_LensDistortion`, `r_LensChromaticAberration` and `r_LensLetterbox`  are **overwritten every  frame** while a cinematic camera drives the render state (from its Vignette / Distortion /  Anamorphic Squeeze properties), so  setting them by hand only sticks while no cinematic camera is active; the plugin restores  the pre-activation values when the camera stops driving.- **Diagnostic**: force modes used to verify that a pipeline stage runs at all, independent of  what the game code feeds it.Naming standard: every depth-of-field cvar spells the family out as `r_DepthOfField*`. Threecvars were renamed to follow it:| Old name | Current name || --- | --- || `r_DofBokehShapeStrength` | `r_DepthOfFieldBokehShapeStrength` || `r_DofSpriteBokehDebug` | `r_DepthOfFieldSpriteBokehDebug` || `r_DofResolutionScale` | `r_DepthOfFieldResolutionScale` |(The C++ members keep their short `CV_r_Dof*` names; only the console-facing names changed.)## Console commands### ccam_slot```ccam_slot <1-9>```Activates the cinematic camera whose **Camera Slot** property equals the given number, whichis the same ownership transfer as the Schematyc `Activate` function. It logs the entity itswitched to (`[CinematicCamera] slot 3 -> 'CamB'`) or reports that no cinematic camera carriesthat slot. Called with no argument it prints its usage line.A Phone Tracker with **Follow Active Camera** enabled follows every switch, re-anchoring onthe new camera and re-sending the last lens state to it.Bind the slots to the number keys to cut between cameras during play:```bind 1 ccam_slot 1bind 2 ccam_slot 2bind 3 ccam_slot 3```The same switch is exported for other plugins as `CryActivateCinematicCameraSlot(int slot)`(see `Interface/ICinematicCameraOptics.h`).## Live tuning### r_DepthOfFieldMode| | || --- | --- || Default | 1 || Purpose | Selects the DOF implementation. 1 = pure gather. 0 = hybrid: the same gather runs first, then a highlight splat layer (sprite bokeh) is added on top of it. |Driven by the component's **Use Sprite Bokeh** property (on -> 0), saved and restored aroundthe camera being active. Setting it to 0 from the console is an equally valid way to reach thehybrid path ??the shape texture follows the effective cvar, not the property.### r_DepthOfFieldBokehShapeStrength| | || --- | --- || Default | 1.0 || Range | input clamped to 0.25-4.0, then the resulting exponent is clamped to 0-1 || Purpose | Reach of the **blade curvature** in the bokeh outline, applied to both the gather taps and the splat's polygon. The iris exponent is `1 - curvature * p * strength`, where `p` is the pupil scale (the opening radius relative to the lens wide open): exponent 0 = perfect circle, 1 = the exact polygon. 1 = physical: a curved blade's arc bulges exactly as far as the opening allows. Above 1 keeps curved blades round further down the stops; below 1 sharpens them into the polygon sooner. |A real iris opening is the intersection of its blades and is therefore always **convex**, sonothing may push its corners past the true polygon. The old 2-4 range did exactly that: theradius function is normalised to the edge midpoints, so a corner sits 8% out for an octagon but**2.0x** out for a triangle, and an exponent of 2 gave that triangle 4x-radius spikes - aconcave cloverleaf instead of an aperture.Not overwritten by the plugin; tune it from the console at any time. Blade Curvature feeds thesame exponent: straight blades (curvature 0) stay the exact polygon at every aperture, curvedblades round off in proportion to how far the iris is open. This replaces the old f/1.2 - f/2.8formation window, which was an approximation of the same curve for a single fast lens andwrongly forced the exact polygon on **every** lens above f/2.8. Curvature's other half is thepolygon's **edge softness** in the splat (0.06 pupil-radius units when fully curved, 0.015 whenfully straight), which is what makes a straight blade read as sharper without deforming thesilhouette.### r_DepthOfFieldResolutionScale| | || --- | --- || Default | 0 || Purpose | Internal resolution of the hybrid splat pass (`r_DepthOfFieldMode 0`). 0 = half-resolution quad grid (cheapest; highlight selection at 2x2 pixel granularity). 1 = native-resolution grid, every native pixel its own splat candidate (sharpest highlight selection; ~4x the DOF vertex work, ~12M vertices at 1080p, early-outs keep the surviving work sparse). 2 = reserved for a native-resolution gather as well; currently behaves as 1. |Splat energy is normalised per grid tier, so the same Highlight Splat Gain deposits the sameenergy at any of these settings.### r_HDRVignetteAmount| | || --- | --- || Default | 0.0 || Range | clamped to 0.0-4.0 in use || Purpose | Strength of the tone map stage's vignette map. 0 = off (default), 1 = the stock map, 2 = deepened. |**Overwritten every frame while a cinematic camera is active** (result of Vignette Amount +Vignette Aperture Link), in edit mode as well as in game mode.### r_LensDistortion| | || --- | --- || Default | 0.0 || Range | clamped to -0.9-0.9 in use || Purpose | Radial lens distortion in the final composite (PostAA). Magnification depends on wavelength, so red is sampled at a slightly larger radial scale than green and blue at a slightly smaller one: high-contrast edges pick up a red/orange fringe on the outer side and a blue/cyan one on the inner, zero at the centre and growing toward the frame edges. Kernel parameter changes are logged as`[DOF] kernel blades=N fNumber=X`, with `(FORCED)` appended in force mode.### r_DepthOfFieldSpriteBokehDebug| | || --- | --- || Default | 0 || Purpose | Diagnostic for the hybrid sprite bokeh splats (`r_DepthOfFieldMode 0`). 1 = every surviving splat is drawn as its raw shape mask at full strength, skipping the energy normalization, so the silhouette (texture x iris polygon) becomes unmissable. 0 = off. |### r_DepthOfFieldSpriteBokehFadeCoC| | || --- | --- || Default | 1.0 || Purpose | Circle of confusion (gather units; 4 px of disc radius, i.e. 8 px of diameter, per unit at 1080p) at which a hybrid sprite bokeh splat reaches full strength. The gather's halo loses energy as a highlight approaches focus while an energy-conserving splat only gets brighter per pixel, so without the fade a small sharp silhouette floats on an already-converged light and then vanishes in a single frame. Raise it to make the cat-eye shape and the mechanical edge darkening reach further in from the edges, lower it to confine them to the extreme corners. |### r_DepthOfFieldLensModel| | || --- | --- || Default | 1 || Purpose | Kill switch for the **lens model** depth of field ([DofLensModelSpec.md](../DofLensModelSpec.md)), which drives focus and blur from the thin lens itself - focal length, f-number, focus distance and sensor - instead of the engine's focus-range ramp, weights the gather by the energy each tap actually scatters onto the pixel, and leaves the depth of field natively sharp. It is a separate shader permutation that runs **only** when the Cinematic Camera component asks for it (`Lens Model DOF`, on by default). 1 = honour the request. 0 = ignore it and always take the stock path, which is then byte for byte the engine's own depth of field - the first thing to try if anything about the blur looks wrong. |### r_DepthOfFieldLensModelMaxCoC| | || --- | --- || Default | 4.0 || Purpose | How far the lens-model **gather kernel** is allowed to reach, in gather units (~4 px of disc radius per unit at 1080p). Not an optical quantity and **not a cap on the lens**: the circle of confusion texture carries the physical blur regardless, and the sprite splats are sized from it - this bounds only the 121-tap half-resolution kernel, which cannot resolve a disc of any size. Past it the gather's halo stops growing and the splat carries the rest. Raising it gives larger halos at the cost of sparser sampling - ringing and visible tap copies in a big disc - which `r_DepthOfFieldLensModelJitter` and the second 3x3 iteration hide. 4.0 is the stock kernel's own reach; ~8 is usable with jitter on. Use `r_DepthOfFieldLensModelDebug 1` to see how far past the cap the lens is actually asking to go. |### r_DepthOfFieldLensModelSplatMaxCoC| | || --- | --- || Default | 256 || Purpose | Largest bokeh sprite the lens-model splat will draw, as a **diameter** in native pixels - 256 means a disc 128 px in radius. It is also the only ceiling the circle of confusion texture takes, so it bounds the physical blur every consumer downstream sees (tile pass, layer alpha, sprite size). A sprite is an *exact* evaluation of the pupil rather than a sparse sample of it, so a large disc costs fill rate and nothing else - hence a cap far above the gather's reach. The consequence to watch for: a very bright point far out of focus shows the gather's capped halo *plus* a larger, fainter exact disc. If that reads wrong, lower this or raise `r_DepthOfFieldLensModelMaxCoC` so the two meet. The stock path is fixed at 64 px and ignores this. |### r_DepthOfFieldLensModelJitter| | || --- | --- || Default | 1.0 || Purpose | How far the lens-model gather may offset each of its 121 taps inside its own cell, as a fraction of the largest offset that still keeps the tap in that cell. A fixed tap grid samples the source at the *same* places in every pixel, so above a few units of reach each tap's bilinear footprint reads as a separate copy of the source and a big disc comes apart into a handful of circles. A per-pixel offset turns that structure into noise, which the temporal AA resolves. The offset is clamped back inside the iris polygon, so only the disc's interior is roughened - the silhouette's outline stays exact. The pattern also advances one golden-ratio step per frame: a jitter that depends on the pixel alone is fixed-pattern noise, and fixed-pattern noise is precisely what temporal AA cannot average away. In the **mask-kernel domain** the amplitude is automatically quartered, because the clamp is a polygon radius and a mask silhouette has none - at full amplitude a tap could be pushed out of the mask's open area and smear the silhouette into the halo. || Values | `0` = the bare stratified grid (brings the tap copies back for an A/B). `0..1` = amplitude, 1 = full. `2` = full amplitude with the per-frame phase **frozen**, i.e. the old static per-pixel pattern - use it to tell tap structure apart from TAA ghosting. |### r_DepthOfFieldLensModelDebug| | || --- | --- || Default | 0 || Purpose | Diagnostic for the lens model. 1 = log the **uncapped** circle of confusion the thin lens is asking for once a second (far at infinity, near at half the focus distance, plus what percentage is being clipped), and paint the composite as a false-colour map: red = how much of the far layer replaced the frame, green = the frame the lens says is sharp, blue = the near layer's pupil opacity.
