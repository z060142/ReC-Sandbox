Why, and the one ruleEverything the cinematic camera added to the depth of field so far ??the field-dependentpupil, the shape mask, axial CA, the sprite splat, the ghosts, the anamorphic oval ??hangs offCrytek's stock skeleton, and four pieces of that skeleton are not optical at all:1. **The circle of confusion** (`ComputeCircleOfConfusion`) is `saturate(depth繚a + b)簡 繚 scale`,   clamped to 簣4: a focus *range* with a quadratic ramp, not a lens. The component has to   reverse-engineer a `FocusRange` and a `BlurAmount` from thin-lens numbers and then fudge the   result with `Focus Falloff` because the ramp saturates exactly where the picture should still   be sharp. Field curvature and edge softness are patches on that fake curve.2. **The gather is an average**, not a sum of light. Every tap is weighted by an ad-hoc   "is the tap at least as blurred as me" heuristic and the accumulation is divided by the   weight sum. A bright point therefore does not get dimmer as its disc grows, taps from a   sharp neighbour leak into a blurred pixel at the wrong strength, and the splat had to be   bolted on with a second, additive energy convention ??plus `Highlight Gain` and the fade-in   cvar to hide the seam between the two conventions.3. **The near / far split** decides occlusion with a tile-min CoC and the same heuristic   weights: partial occlusion at a foreground edge is approximate.4. **The composite** upsamples the half-resolution layers bicubically and lerps them over the   sharp frame by `saturate(CoC)`, where CoC = 1 is a 4-pixel radius. A pixel that is 2 px out   of focus is already a 25 % blend of a half-resolution blur: the focus zone is never truly   sharp.This round replaces the *mathematics inside* those four pieces with a lens model, keepingevery pass, target and constant the pipeline already has. The visible payoff: focus, blur sizeand near/far behaviour that follow focal length, f-number, focus distance and sensor exactly;bokeh discs whose brightness is right relative to each other so the splat becomes a refinementinstead of a second system; a genuinely sharp focus zone; and a shared pupil function everyconsumer (gather, splat, ghost, later the FFT glare) evaluates identically, with one new lensparameter (spherical aberration) that only a shared pupil function could give.**The one rule (the user's, 2026-08-27): this is ccam-exclusive.** The lens model runs onlywhen the Cinematic Camera component asks for it. With the component off, disabled, or its`Lens Model DOF` switch off ??and whenever the kill-switch cvar is 0 ??every DOF shader andevery CPU path must be **byte-identical to `dev`**: the stock permutation of each technique isnot edited, the stock `ComputeCircleOfConfusion` is not edited, the stock weights are notedited. The lens model is a separate shader permutation and separate CPU branches. If anythingin it is wrong, the engine is still an ordinary CRYENGINE.## 1.
